Speaker notes

The conceptual hinge between protocol world and code world. Walk the ladder: a macro/enum symbol or numeric-id hit is EXACT; a normalized-name hit is NORMALIZED; a substring/token overlap is HEURISTIC_SUBSTRING; a function-name resemblance with no id is NAME_ONLY. Everything the calculus later does with “match strength” is grounded here. It’s bidirectional: the KB produces the canonical identity, the code site produces a raw identifier, and matching scores their correspondence.

Stress semantic equivalence vs syntactic divergence. The designated and positional aggregate forms are semantically identical but structurally different in the AST (designated members are wrapped assignments). This diversity is exactly why one universal matcher would be brittle.

Prior weight is per kind and dispatch signals rank as high as registration — a dispatch match is not a weak signal; the genuinely weak kind is name-only matching. There is no rule reserving resolution for structural kinds.

EVIDENCE COMBINATION12 / 20
Evidence Combination

Collapse duplicates first, then combine independents

Within-site MAX removes duplicates; cross-site noisy-OR then rewards corroboration.

Recognition›Evidence›Calculus›Decision
many scored evidences for one candidate
step 1 · collapse
within a site : MAXsame site ≠ new information
step 2 · combine
across sites : noisy-ORindependent corroboration ↑ belief
Weak-only Cap · Global Cap · Conflict Penalty
candidate confidence
Collapse: same-site duplicates reduce to their MAX — a site can't inflate itself
Combine: only then do independent sites corroborate (noisy-OR)
Weak-only cap keys on match strength (missing exact-id, any kind); competitors penalized
Speaker notes

The intellectual core. Why noisy-OR: independent corroboration should raise belief but never reach certainty (a Global Cap keeps confidence below 1.0). Why within-site MAX: two records from the same registration are not two witnesses. The Weak-only Cap keys on match strength, not evidence kind: a candidate with no exact-identifier match is capped, so a stack of weak-match signals can’t masquerade as strong — but a dispatch signal with an exact match is not capped.

Speaker notes

The deliberate-abstention point. A wrong resolution silently corrupts every downstream stage, so when evidence is genuinely split the correct answer is to refuse and hand the conflict to F6/a human. AMBIGUOUS is a feature. The gate is Floor + Margin only: the weak-only cap lowers a non-exact-match candidate’s confidence but does not by itself prevent it from resolving, and a strongly-matched dispatch signal can and does resolve on its own.

Match strength picks the winner, not kind

Two extractors fire; the stronger identifier match resolves — here, dispatch.

remove the switch → only A → foo resolves alone (≈0.56, just clears the floor)
Two independent extractors, same action id, different callbacks (registration vs dispatch)
The dispatch evidence matched more strongly → bar clears the margin → resolves to bar
The decision is a function of evidence and match strength, not of kind
Speaker notes

Real numbers: bar (ENUM_CASE) prior 0.85 × normalized-name match (×0.85) ≈ 0.72; foo (REGISTRATION_INIT) prior 0.80 × heuristic-substring match (×0.70) ≈ 0.56. The gap ≈0.16 clears the Ambiguity Margin, so the tool RESOLVES to bar — the dispatch handler — over the structural registration. Resolution is match-strength-driven and kind-agnostic; there is no structural authorization. To get AMBIGUOUS you'd need two candidates within the margin.

Evaluation & Conclusions

Separating recognition from decision is the contribution

Capability and regression tests pin behavior; the backbone makes it extensible.

supported forms→RESOLVED (kind, confidence)
ambiguous forms→AMBIGUOUS (retain, margin)
unsupported forms→UNRESOLVED (named reason)
Recognition
Evidence
Calculus
Decision
“By separating recognition from decision through an evidence abstraction, F2-A becomes conservative, auditable, and naturally extensible.”
Each registration mechanism has a fixture asserting its exact expected outcome
Negative & unsupported cases assert the right refusal, not just “no crash”
The backbone — Recognition → Evidence → Calculus → Decision — is the contribution
Speaker notes

Test philosophy: capability tests prove a mechanism works; regression tests prevent silent drift; unsupported-pattern tests assert the diagnosis. Close on the architecture: conservative through confidence thresholds, conflict handling, provenance-aware combination, and explicit abstention — not through any structural-evidence gate; auditable because every resolution traces to evidence records; extensible because new extractors feed the same evidence bus without touching the calculus or the decision logic.
